# Partner logo sources

- `acm-ucf.svg`: ACM & ACM-W at UCF. Derived from the existing
  `apps/2025/public/partnersSection/acm.svg`. The original vector wordmark,
  diamond, and infinity silhouette are retained; the raster clipped inside the
  infinity silhouette is replaced with a solid fill for the monochrome partner
  treatment. The diamond's transform is flattened without changing its geometry.
  Organization: <https://ucf.acm.org/>.
- `shpe-ucf.png`: Society of Hispanic Professional Engineers at UCF. Original
  transparent 2396 × 434 PNG from the chapter's official website:
  <https://www.shpeucf.com/assets/NavLogo.png>.
  Organization: <https://www.shpeucf.com/>.
- `rccf.png`: Robotics Club of Central Florida. Original transparent 375 × 400 PNG
  from the club's official website:
  <https://rccf.club/assets/rccf-logo-CZtd0nVt.png>.
  The same asset is maintained in the club's official repository:
  <https://github.com/RoboticsClubatUCF/Robotics-Club-Website/blob/main/web/src/assets/rccf-logo.png>.
  Organization: <https://rccf.club/>. This is the brain-and-gear mark in the supplied
  partner reference.

Sources checked September 12, 2026. These assets belong to their respective
organizations; inclusion here does not transfer trademark ownership.

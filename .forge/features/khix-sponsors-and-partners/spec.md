# Knight Hacks IX Sponsors and Partners

Status: Implementation

## Purpose and scope

Add the sponsors and community partners in the user's roster graphic to the
public KHIX homepage. Preserve the floating rocks, waterfall, and team section.

## Requirements

- Add Hugging Face, NVIDIA, UCF Student Government, GitHub, and Bloomberg.
- Omit Lovable for now at the user's request; retain its asset for later reuse.
- Add ACM & ACM-W at UCF, SHPE UCF, and Robotics Club of Central Florida (RCCF).
- Show the Codex icon and wordmark together, matching the reference.
- Keep logos readable, centered, and contained on rocks on desktop and mobile.
- Keep all current sponsors/partners and their external links.
- Keep the final partner row clear of the Team heading and profile cascade.

## Placement assumption

Preserve current tiers. Provisionally place Hugging Face beside OneEthos,
NVIDIA in the middle group, and UCF SG/GitHub/Bloomberg in the smaller
group, following visual prominence in the reference. This is a display decision,
not a claim about sponsorship amounts. Clarification was requested.

## Non-goals

No sponsor administration, other apps, portal behavior, or deployment changes.

## Confirmed partner identity

The brain-and-gear mark is RCCF. Its official website at <https://rccf.club/>
uses the exact mark in the supplied graphic, also maintained in the club's
[official website repository](https://github.com/RoboticsClubatUCF/Robotics-Club-Website/blob/main/web/src/assets/rccf-logo.png).
AI@UCF was an initial unconfirmed guess and is not part of this addition.

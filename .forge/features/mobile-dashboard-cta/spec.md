# Mobile Dashboard CTA Spec

Status: Approved scope from the user's screenshot and instructions (2026-10-08).

## User-facing purpose

Make the Knight Hacks IX dashboard easy to discover and the homepage's primary action readable on mobile.

## Users / actors

Public visitors and returning hackers on the 2026 website.

## User-visible interface

Keep the illustrated forest, event logo, and typography. Raise the foreground grass that flows into About on mobile, retaining a clear view of TK and more purple canopy as requested in follow-up feedback. Lower the hero button onto that foreground, give its text reliable contrast, and label it “Log into Dashboard” on mobile and desktop. Open the existing dashboard route.

## Scope

Only the 2026 homepage hero and foreground positioning. Preserve existing email work on `emailfix`, as explicitly requested. Authentication, dashboard behavior, other apps, and artwork files are outside this change.

## Acceptance criteria

- Mobile foreground and CTA are both 10svh higher than the lowered review revision, following the final placement request.
- The primary button sits lower and over the foreground with readable text.
- The complete “Log into Dashboard” label fits at 320px and links to the dashboard.
- Touch target is at least 44px tall, with visible keyboard focus.
- The About transition remains continuous and desktop composition is preserved.

None needed for this bounded visual adjustment.
